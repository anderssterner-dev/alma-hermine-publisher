Alma & Hermine Publishing Studio

A private desktop application used only by the owner of the Alma and Hermine pages to prepare and publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel.

It is not offered to other people, has no user accounts and no web service. Everything runs on the owner's own Windows computer.
